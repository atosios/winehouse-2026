About us

A house built on corks & conversations

The Winehouse began the way most good things do — around a table, long after the plates were cleared, when someone opened one more bottle "just to try". What started as a shelf of favourites for friends slowly grew into a cellar, then a shop, then a small world of its own.

We are not sommeliers in suits. We are people who fell for wine sideways — through harvest trips, through winemakers who talk about their vines like family, through bottles that tasted like the hillside they came from. That is the wine we look for, and the only kind we sell.

Every bottle on our shelves has been opened, argued about, and loved by at least one of us.

Beyond the shelves, The Winehouse is a place for stories: tasting notes and grape lore, vineyard tours and long evenings, facts and myths and everything poured in between. Whether you are a first-glass beginner or a seasoned cellar-rat, pull up a chair — you are already one of us.
